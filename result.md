# Prompt Testing Results
## Original Prompt — Version 1.0
Write an Instagram caption about artificial intelligence for students.
The original prompt was evaluated against five different test scenarios to identify its strengths, weaknesses, and instruction-following limitations.
## Test Case 1 — General AI Topic
### Input
Create an Instagram caption about the benefits of artificial intelligence for students.

### Expected Result
- Relevant to students
- Explains benefits of AI
- Clear and engaging
- Suitable for Instagram

### Actual Result
**AI + Students = Smarter Learning 🤖📚**

Artificial Intelligence can make student life easier, smarter, and more productive! 🚀

✨ **Learn faster** with personalized explanations
🔍 **Research smarter** by finding and organizing information
💻 **Improve coding skills** with AI-assisted practice
📝 **Boost productivity** with writing and study support
💡 **Spark creativity** for projects and presentations
🎯 **Save time** on repetitive tasks and focus on what matters

AI isn’t here to replace learning—it’s here to **help you learn better.** 🌱

#AIForStudents #ArtificialIntelligence #StudentLife #AITools #StudySmart #FutureSkills #TechStudents


### Evaluation
The output is relevant to students and clearly describes multiple benefits of AI. It is engaging and formatted appropriately for Instagram.
**Result:** Pass
**Reason:**
The output met the expected criteria because it clearly explains multiple benefits of AI for students and uses an engaging Instagram-friendly format.
